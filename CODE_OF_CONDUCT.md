# Code of conduct

tjforge follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). In short:

## Expected

- Be kind and patient, especially with people who are new to Claude Code or git.
- Disagree about the work, not the person.
- Give feedback that someone can act on.
- Accept that the maintainer may decline a change for scope.

## Not accepted

- Harassment, insults or personal attacks.
- Sexual language or imagery, and unwelcome attention.
- Publishing someone's private information.
- Spam, or repeatedly pushing the same request after it was declined.

## Where it applies

Issues, pull requests, discussions and anywhere else you represent the project.

## Reporting

Report a problem privately to the maintainer through the [GitHub profile](https://github.com/tjthouhid), or with a private report from the repo's **Security** tab if it involves sensitive details. Reports are kept confidential.

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, close threads, or block anyone who breaks these rules, in line with the Contributor Covenant's enforcement guidelines.
